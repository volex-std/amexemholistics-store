What I Found in the Store
🏠
Homepage has no premium feel and no direction
No clear hook, no reviews, no testimonials, no proper product listings. Nothing that gives a visitor a reason to stay or trust the brand. First impressions either close the sale or end it.
Critical
📄
Product pages are not building any trust
Descriptions are clearly AI written. Long paragraphs, no structure, no personality. One review on the whole store. No indication of how many people have bought. Images in irregular sizes make the page feel unfinished. People could see the product and still leave.
Critical
🗂️
Collection pages are basically empty
Click into a category and there's one product staring back at you. That's not a collection, that's a dead end. Customers need to browse, compare, and explore before they commit to buying.
Critical
✍️
Blog page has placeholder text still live
Lorem ipsum. Sample block quotes. Empty bullet points. The blog was never properly set up and it's still live, visible to every person Pinterest sends over. This kills credibility before they even read a word.
Critical
🔗
Blog Is Invisible From the Homepage
Even if the blog had great content, there's no way to reach it from the homepage. It's not in the navigation. Customers who came to explore have nowhere to go.
Major
⏱️
Every Second of Attention Is Leaking
People came because Pinterest gave them a reason to. They had intent. But without trust signals, clean pages, and a clear path, they left. Every one of those 14,000 sessions was a real person who wanted to buy.
Major

We shouldn't try META ads yet.

I was wrong when I said you need the right audience. You already had it. 14,000 people showed up and they had intent. The same thing will happen on META if we go there now — good money, same result.

I'm not sending this to pitch you or sell you anything. I just spent my whole day on this and I genuinely think these are the things bleeding you. The leaks need to be fixed before more traffic comes in — because right now the store isn't ready to close what Pinterest already opened.

I know this store inside out at this point. I know what it needs and I know how to get it there. But that's not why I wrote this. I wrote this because it's worth fixing.
